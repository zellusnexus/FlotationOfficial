    The Beaumont Series

    The Beaumont Series is our signature dock design with an elegant Southern architectural style.

    Arched supports and curved balusters create a warm appeal that welcomes friends and family to a relaxing day on the lake.

    • Integrated arched supports, for open dock space and expanded scenic views
    • Sundeck balusters, available in straight picket or graceful curved options
    • Warm earth tone color palette to compliment natural landscapes and homesites
    • Optional cupola with louvers or four-pane glass windows, and with optional weathervane
