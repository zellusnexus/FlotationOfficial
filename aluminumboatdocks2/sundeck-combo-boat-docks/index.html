    Let's Dock

    Our sundeck combo boat docks take the best of a traditional sundeck and combines it with the protection of a dock roof. You gain 20 extra feet of dock space plus a sloped dock roof that keeps you, your boat, and all your outdoor items safe from the elements.

    And speaking of safety, our sundeck combo docks surpass all national building codes. We craft them from waterproof LockDry aluminum marine decking and powder coated alumium fencing, making them as safe and durable as they are attractive.

    When you add dock options like lockable gates, corner benches, cable railing and custom waterslides, your sundeck combo boat dock will soon become everyone's favorite escape on the water.
