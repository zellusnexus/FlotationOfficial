    How to Order

    LockDry® Aluminum Marine Decking in three easy steps -

    SKETCH
    Using our Decking Design Form, sketch out the perimeter…

    DETAIL
    Include colors, styles, and any special notes…

    SEND
    When you’re done, send it back. It’s that simple.

    Get Started

    Sketch

    Download our provided Decking Design Form. Fill it out online or print and sketch the outer perimeter of your boat dock, pier, boardwalk or anywhere your decking will be applied. Be sure to add dimensions to any fingers, slips or platforms.

    To make things more streamlined, add a drawing scale. This ensures that you receive an accurate quote from our design and sales teams.

    You can also give us a call and we will be more than happy to fill out your design form for you over the phone.

    Get Started

    Detail

    Choose from one of our various decking styles and colors (color choices only apply to LockDry® Aluminum Marine decking)

    – For stairs, include total number of steps as well as the step width and length
    – If you know your drawing scale, be sure to note that in the provided box above the design grid.
    – Include any and all special that you need us to consider. This eliminates any confusion that may arise in the quoting process.

    To view our available color choices, view the main LockDry®marine decking page HERE.

    Send

    The third and final step is simple. Add your contact and shipping information and send it back to us via fax or email.

    Fax: (256) 287-0417
    Email: info@aluminumboatdocks.com | melanie@aluminumboatdocks.com

    Quote Turnaround

    Once your design form is sent, we send a confirmation via email ensuring you that we received your information. An accurate quote will be returned to you within 48 hours. If you do not hear from us, please give us a call to make sure everything went through. If you have any other questions, give us a call at 1-800-711-1785.

    Payment Methods

    We accept all major credit cards, wire transfers, and checks (all checks must clear before shipment). If you are paying by credit card, please fill out and sign the credit card authorization form that was sent with your quote.

    Lead Times

    Once you have received and looked over your quote, sign and fax or email back to us, and we’ll begin preparing your order. Our lead times officially begin once we have received a signed quote from you. For current lead times on both standard and special orders contact us directly.

    Still have questions? Send us a message!
