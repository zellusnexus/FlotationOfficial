    Boat Dock Color Choices

    The full spectrum of beauty

    Create a floating masterpiece in the colors of your dreams. Cool to brilliant, earth tones and primary colors, we have the perfect dock color choice for your style.

    We are proud to offer eighteen beautiful and durable roof colors, seven standard railing colors, and four deck color choices to perfectly match your home or make a distinctive statement. Custom color choices are available for railings, frames, ramps and posts, using the Sherwin Williams RAL Colors collection (see Powder Coated Finishes below).

    Roof colors are baked-on, ultra-durable enamel with a 40-year limited paint warranty. Railing and decking colors are high-density polyester powder-coat finish up to three times thicker than conventional finishes. Decking finish is a textured, non-skid finish.

    If you need more information for your decision, samples are available on request; please contact us.

    For stunning ready-made color combinations, please also see our Designer Color Series docks.

    Powder Coated Finishes

    Our finish is the beginning of beautiful, lasting color.

    Powder coat provides a deep, colorful finish without compromise. Perfectly beautiful, amazingly durable finishes are available in an almost endless variety of colors and textures. Custom color configurations are available for railing, frames, ramps and posts, using the Sherwin Williams RAL Colors collection. (Decking and roof metal choices are not available in custom colors. See options in Roof and Decking Colors above.)

    Powder coat finishes are highly resistant to all forms of wear, including scratching, fading, chipping and corrosion. Powder coats are chemical resistant and good electrical insulators due to the thick coverage.

    Electrostatic powder coating results in a coverage up to and often exceeding 4x that of conventionally applied wet paint. They are consistent, uniform, and free from common wet paint flaws. The finish retains its color and gloss over time, and a vast variety of colors, finishes, and textures are available without the need for special painting techniques.

    Unlike paint, powder is free from volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and polluting solvents for a more environmentally-friendly product.

    We contract with PowderTec, Inc. for the highest architectural powder coat finish available. Meets AAMA 2604 certification.
